About Our Foundation

Learn about our history, core values, and vision for sustainable communities.

Our History

Founded in 2021, EcoReach Foundation started as a small neighborhood clean-up group in Cape Town. Driven by a shared commitment to protect open natural spaces, the initiative rapidly expanded into a recognized regional environmental organization serving communities across South Africa.

Through grassroots organizing and corporate partnerships, we have facilitated hundreds of cleanup operations, diverted metric tons of waste from landfills, and educated thousands of students.

Mission Statement

To empower local communities through practical sustainability education, clean-up initiatives, and direct conservation action that yields measurable, long-term environmental protection.

Vision Statement

To build environmentally resilient urban communities operating with zero landfill waste, thriving green spaces, and fully engaged eco-conscious citizens.

Who We Serve

Our initiatives are designed to bring together diverse groups within society to foster unified environmental responsibility:

Local Residents

Providing households with functional recycling guidance and regular community cleanup events.

Schools & Youth

Empowering the next generation with environmental literacy, outdoor learning, and sustainability projects.

Sponsors & Partners

Connecting corporate sustainability goals with tangible, high-impact community projects.

EcoReach Foundation

Building resilient, sustainable, and zero-landfill communities across South Africa.

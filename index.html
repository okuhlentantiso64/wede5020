Welcome to EcoReach Foundation

At EcoReach Foundation, we believe that sustainable environmental change begins at the grassroots level. Established to combat urban environmental degradation, our mission is to empower communities across South Africa through education, organized cleanup drives, and structured recycling frameworks.

Explore our site to discover how you can participate, donate, or volunteer to safeguard our natural ecosystems for generations to come.

Our Core Focus Areas

Community Recycling

Developing practical recycling sorting systems and localized composting centers to reduce landfill waste.

Urban Cleanup

Mobilizing dedicated volunteers for monthly cleanup drives across public parks, rivers, and coastal lines.

Youth Education

Conducting interactive workshops and tree-planting events across schools to foster future eco-leaders.

EcoReach Foundation

Building resilient, sustainable, and zero-landfill communities across South Africa.
